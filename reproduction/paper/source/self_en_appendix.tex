This appendix gives the input that each model receives for one item: the first of the 150 bodies, with the attribution question in its first option order, rendered by the model's own chat template in each template setting of the runs. Every rendering was produced from the model's tokenizer and template files at the revision used in its runs (Appendix~\ref{app:config}) and agrees with the template's tokenized output, as checked in the runs. \PH{body}, \PH{question}, and \PH{request} stand for the body, the question, and the request that opens the natural order (``Please write a short description.''), in the language in which the model is tested (Table~\ref{tab:self_items} gives the English texts, and the Chinese ones will be released with the code). The symbol \NL{} marks a line feed, and every other character is the template's output. The gpt-oss template inserts the day of rendering (\PH{date}). The token names of the DeepSeek models contain the characters U+FF5C and U+2581, printed here as | and \_. Models whose templates render identically in every setting share one block. Where the three renderings of a block begin with the same long text, it is printed once, on the line Start, and stands as \PH{start} in each rendering. A line break without \NL{} only wraps the line.
